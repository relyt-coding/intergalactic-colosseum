Your browser isn't using your graphics card

It's drawing the 3D on the processor instead, which is why it's slow. To fix it:

  1. Chrome / Edge: Settings → System → turn on "Use graphics acceleration when available", then restart the browser.
  2. Windows: Settings → System → Display → Graphics → add your browser → High performance.
  3. Check chrome://gpu: "WebGL" should say Hardware accelerated.
